Antropolis privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Antropolis is a game made by Antropolis Gamez, a small independent studio in the United Kingdom. This page covers the game on the web and the Antropolis app.

The short version: there are no accounts, we never ask who you are, and your colony stays on your device. The game sends a few anonymous "this happened" counts so we can see where players get stuck, and you can switch that off.

What stays on your device

Your colony, your settings and your progress are saved on your own phone, tablet or computer. They are not sent to us. If you clear the game's data or uninstall the app, the save is deleted. You can keep your own backup from the Menu.

Anonymous play stats

The game sends small anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a privacy-focused statistics service (goatcounter.com). They tell us things like:

  • the game was opened, and whether it was opened again after 1, 2, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days
  • how long the game has been played in total on a device, in broad steps (1 minute, 10 minutes, an hour and so on)
  • which stages, goals and milestones were reached
  • whether you are playing on the web, from the home screen or in the Android app, and the game version
  • if the game hit an error, the build number and the line it happened on

Each count is sent once per device. They contain no name, no email, no account, no advertising ID and none of your save. The game does not use cookies for this. As with any internet request, GoatCounter's servers see your IP address while the request is made.

You can turn this off at any time: open the Menu in the game and switch off Anonymous play stats.

Fonts

The game loads its lettering from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives a normal web request from your device, which includes your IP address. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Feedback

If you use Send feedback in the Menu, the game opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you press send. We then receive your email address and whatever you wrote, along with the game version and basic device details shown in the message. We use that only to read and reply to your feedback.

Adverts and purchases

This version of Antropolis shows no third-party adverts and has no purchases. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Children

Antropolis does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Turn off anonymous play stats in the Menu.
  • Erase everything saved on your device with Erase everything in the game, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Ask us to delete any feedback email you have sent us.
